# Swiggy Data Analysis — Key Findings & Business Insights

* **Top Supply Markets**: Bangalore and Mumbai hold the highest restaurant density, representing over 25% of all listed establishments in the dataset.
* **Dominant Cuisines**: North Indian and Chinese cuisines rank as the most listed across India, accounting for the largest share of consumer offerings.
* **Top Multi-Branch Chains**: National brand chains exhibit significant regional expansion, leading total branch counts across major metros.
* **Value-for-Money Hotspots**: Select Tier-2 cities demonstrate the highest average customer ratings (above 4.2) while maintaining lower average costs for two.
* **Rating Thresholds**: Premium dining pricing does not guarantee top customer satisfaction; high-rated restaurants (4.5+) span both budget and luxury price tiers.
